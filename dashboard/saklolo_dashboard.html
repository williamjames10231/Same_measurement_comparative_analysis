About S.A.K.L.O.L.O.

v1.0.0

Semantic Algebraic Kernel for Localized Operational Logistics and Optimization

An automated Natural Language Processing (NLP) dashboard designed to classify the sentiment and severity of unstructured, mixed-syntax (Taglish) emergency text feeds during typhoons and floods to assist local DRRM operations.

Primary Architecture
Hybrid Pipeline (LDA + RoBERTa) vs. LLM Baseline (Qwen 3.5)
Corpus Size
10,194 Emergency Reports
Temporal Scope
2015 – 2025
Methodology
Comparative Quantitative Evaluative Design

Evaluation Framework & Metrics

Statistical Measures

  • RQ1 (Classification): Precision, Recall, Macro-F1, Cohen's Kappa
  • RQ2 (Joint Matrix): Chi-Square Statistic, Cramer's V
  • RQ3 (Utility): Agreement Rate, McNemar's Test

Blind Stakeholder Panel (n=10-20)

  • Cohort A: Bulacan & CAMANAVA DRRM Operational Officers
  • Cohort B: Academic & Technical Experts (Applied Math & CS)
